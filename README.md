### About Me

Electrical engineer based in **Winnipeg, Canada** with deep expertise at the intersection of **embedded systems**, **wireless communications**, and **AI/ML**. I design and build end-to-end systems — from bare-metal firmware on microcontrollers to 5G physical-layer algorithms and edge-deployed machine learning models. My work spans the full stack of intelligent wireless systems: FPGA & MCU hardware, real-time DSP, communication protocol design, and on-device AI inference.

I am actively seeking opportunities in **Embedded AI**, **Wireless/5G Systems**, and **Edge Intelligence** where I can contribute to building the next generation of smart, connected devices.
